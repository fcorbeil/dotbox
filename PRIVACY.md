# Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

DotBox is a media player for Windows and Xbox. This policy says what the app does with your information.

## In short

DotBox does not collect, sell or share personal information. It has no advertising, no analytics and no account of its own, and it sends nothing to its developer.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter or choose in the app is kept on your device only:

- **Your sources**: the address of an M3U playlist, or the server, user name and password of an Xtream account. They are kept in the protected credential storage of Windows.
- **Your PIN codes**, kept in the same protected storage, in a form that cannot be read back.
- **Your settings, profiles, favorites, last channel and viewing progress.**
- **Copies of your channel lists and programme guide**, kept so the app starts faster.

Removing the app, or using Settings > Reset in the app, erases all of it.

## What the app connects to

- **Your own sources.** To show channels, movies, series, the programme guide and their pictures, the app connects to the addresses given by the sources you added. Those providers can see your connection (for example your IP address and what you play), under their own terms. DotBox has no link with them.
- **The Microsoft Store.** The app asks the Store whether the full version was purchased, and the purchase itself is handled by Microsoft, under the Microsoft privacy statement. The app never sees your payment details.

The app connects to nothing else.

## Your Windows or Xbox account name

When profiles are first set up, the app may read the name of the person signed in on the device, only to name the first profile. That name stays on the device. If the system refuses, the profile is simply named "Principal".

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
